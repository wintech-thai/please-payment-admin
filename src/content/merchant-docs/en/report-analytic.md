# Report & Analytic

If the [Overview](/documents/merchant/overview) page answers "how's business overall?", this page answers more detailed questions like "which day sold the best?" or "which payer transfers money in most often?" — and lets you download the data to work with further.

## What you can do here

### View the overview and daily charts

The summary cards at the top give totals for the entire selected time range. The two charts below help you see day-by-day trends — the first chart compares Pay-In / Pay-Out / Withdrawal amounts for each day, and the second breaks down the fees paid each day by type.

### View the table broken down by day and merchant

The "Detail by Merchant" table breaks down every figure (Pay-In/Pay-Out/Withdrawal amounts and each type of fee) by day, with a total row for the currently displayed page at the bottom. Click **Export** to download the current page's data as an Excel file.

### Analyze payers (Payer Summary)

This section aggregates data by the name of each Payer who has transferred money into your store during the selected time range — showing the number of transfers, the total amount transferred, and the first and most recent transfer dates. Use the search box to filter down to a specific name, then click Export to download the filtered list. Use this to identify repeat customers or see who transfers money in most often.
